Encly is an encrypted notes app for private notes and tasks that never leave your phone. Offline notes with a PIN lock: no account, no cloud, no ads, and no internet permission at all.

Most note apps want an account and a server. Encly has neither. Your encrypted notes live in one place: your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no ads, no in-app purchases.
• Truly offline: Encly does not request the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your notes anywhere.
• Encrypted notes at rest: a SQLCipher (AES-256) database under a random 256-bit key.
• PIN lock every time you open it: a 6-digit PIN, plus optional fingerprint or face unlock (strong biometrics only).
• Locks itself: after you leave (15 seconds by default, or immediately) or the moment the screen turns off, the database is closed and the key is wiped from memory.
• Screenshots, screen recording and recent-apps previews are blocked. Android cloud backup is off.

RECOVERY PHRASE AND ENCRYPTED BACKUPS
• Optional 12-word recovery phrase: forgot your PIN? The phrase unlocks your vault and lets you set a new one.
• Export all notes, tasks and tags to one encrypted file (AES-256-GCM) and save it wherever you choose: a folder, a USB stick, your own cloud drive. Encly never uploads it.
• Restore on a new phone with the same 12 words, or merge a backup into an existing vault.
• Nobody can recover your notes for you, not even the developer. Keep the words on paper.

A SECURE NOTEPAD THAT IS PLEASANT TO WRITE IN
• Block editor: text, headings, quotes, checklists, bulleted and numbered lists, links and separators.
• Tags, search, list or grid view, several sort orders.
• Tasks with priorities, right next to your notes.
• Trash with restore.
• Five themes (Paper, Forest, Ocean, Graphite, Midnight), light, dark or system mode, dynamic color on Android 12+, and three font sets bundled in the app.

OPEN SOURCE
• Encly is open source (Apache-2.0): anyone can check what it does.
• Works without Google Play Services. Android 8.0 or newer.
• 9 languages: English, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Portuguese and Dutch. Pick one for Encly alone in Settings → Language.
