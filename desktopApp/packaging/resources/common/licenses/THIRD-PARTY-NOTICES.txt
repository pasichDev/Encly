Encly for the desktop: third-party software
===========================================

The libraries and fonts that every Encly build contains (Kotlin, Compose Multiplatform, Okio, SQLCipher, ...) are
listed in the app under About -> Licenses.

    pdfbox-LICENSE.txt, pdfbox-NOTICE.txt

    jna-LICENSE.txt, jna-AL2.0.txt, jna-LGPL2.1.txt
