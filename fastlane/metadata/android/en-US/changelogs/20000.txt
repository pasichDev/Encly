2.0.0: first public release. Offline encrypted vault with a mandatory 6-digit PIN, optional biometric-bound unlock and an optional 12-word recovery phrase. The key is wiped when the app goes to the background. Encrypted backup export and restore keyed by the recovery phrase. No sharing or clipboard export of notes. Fonts bundled, no Google Play Services needed. UI in 9 languages with an in-app language picker.
